H_DynaView
Dynamic View
#
H_DynaView.Help
Click a drawing view (must be a general view with no child views), then hold the left mouse button and drag around the view center to rotate it freely; release to apply, or press Esc to restore the original orientation. Zoom/pan are disabled during the drag, as expected.

H_TolBrush
Tolerance Brush
#
H_TolBrush.Help
Pick a source dimension to read its tolerance settings (tolerance type, upper/lower values, ISO/DIN tolerance table, decimals), then click any number of other dimensions to apply the same settings to each; middle button or Esc finishes. To see tolerances, the Creo config option tol_display must be yes.
