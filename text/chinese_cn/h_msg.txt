选择一个工程图视图（普通视图），选好后在「选择」对话框点「确定」；点「取消」或按 Esc 退出。
#
DYNAVIEW_DRAG
View selected. Just move the mouse to rotate the view freely (X and Y angles): move left/right to turn around the vertical axis, up/down around the horizontal axis. Middle button = done, Esc = cancel and restore.
视图已选中：直接移动鼠标即可全方位旋转视图（水平移动=绕竖直轴转，垂直移动=绕水平轴转）；中键=确定完成，Esc=取消并恢复。

H_DynaView
Dynamic View
视图动态调整
#
H_DynaView.Help
Click a drawing view (must be a general view with no child views), then hold the left mouse button and drag around the view center to rotate it freely; release to apply, or press Esc to restore the original orientation. Zoom/pan are disabled during the drag, as expected.
点击一个工程图视图（须为普通视图且无子视图），按住鼠标左键绕视图中心自由拖动即可旋转该视图；松开左键提交旋转，按 Esc 可恢复原始朝向。拖动过程中缩放/平移按预期关闭。
#

H_TolBrush
Tolerance Brush
公差刷子
#
H_TolBrush.Help
Pick a source dimension to read its tolerance settings (tolerance type, upper/lower values, ISO/DIN tolerance table, decimals), then click any number of other dimensions to apply the same settings to each; middle button or Esc finishes. To see tolerances, the Creo config option tol_display must be yes.
点选一个源尺寸读取它的公差设置（公差类型、上/下公差值、ISO/DIN 公差表、小数位），然后逐个点选其它尺寸把同一套设置刷过去，个数不限；中键或 Esc 结束。要让公差显示出来，Creo 的 config 选项 tol_display 需为 yes。
