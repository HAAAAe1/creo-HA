H_AddBalloon.Help
Click positions on the drawing to place balloons; the number increments automatically (1,2,3...). Keep clicking to place several in a row; middle/right button or Esc finishes. Zoom and pan work as usual while picking.
在工程图上点击位置放置球标，编号自动递增（1、2、3…），可连续点击连续放置；按中键、右键或 Esc 结束。拾取过程中滚轮缩放、中键平移照常可用
#
H_InsertBalloon
Insert Balloon
插入球标
#
H_InsertBalloon.Help
Insert a balloon in the middle of the sequence: click the placement point, then click an existing balloon. The new balloon takes that number and every following number shifts up by one.
插入球标并顺延序号：先点放置位置，再点一个已有球标；新球标使用该编号，其后所有序号自动 +1
#

请输入导出文件的名称（将用于 STP / PDF / DWG 三个文件），按回车确认，Esc 取消。

H_ExportFiles.Help
One click to export: the drawing's 3D model as STEP, the drawing itself as PDF and DWG, all renamed to the name you type (files go to the current working directory).
一键导出：把工程图关联的 3D 模型导出为 STEP，把工程图本身导出为 PDF 和 DWG，三个文件统一用你输入的名字命名（生成在 Creo 当前工作目录）。
